## The path behind the work

**Customer Support** put me close to recurring user friction. It taught me to separate a one-off complaint from a system problem, recognize patterns across many cases, and see how process decisions land on the person doing the work.

**AI Solutions Operations** grew from that foundation. I helped move AI work beyond the initial pilot: building feedback and evaluation loops, shaping internal AI and automation workflows, supporting adoption, and connecting operating evidence to wider decisions.

**Enterprise Product Ownership** brings those pieces together. I now work across discovery, process modeling, product priorities, stakeholder alignment, delivery, evaluation, governance, adoption, and continuous improvement for enterprise workflows.

Outside my primary role, I bootstrapped and lead **The AI Fellowship Madrid Chapter**, creating a local, human-first space for practical learning, responsible experimentation, and better conversations about how AI changes work.

## What I am interested in

- Enterprise product ownership for consequential internal workflows
- AI automation grounded in a real process and measurable outcome
- Evaluation, governance, and human control beyond the happy path
- Adoption and enablement as product work, not launch communications
- Small, inspectable systems that improve how people decide and work

## What I'm building

### [Skills for Better Work](https://github.com/luka-duj/skills-for-better-work)

AI skills for PMs and workflow owners who need a clearer decision before committing to a tool or automation. **Better Work Loop** helps both sides understand the problem, decide whether anything should be built, shape the smallest useful test, build only that slice, examine the evidence, and prepare the next decision.

This is a public alpha, built and tested natively in Codex. The same six skill folders can be imported into Claude Code with small changes to the install path, invocation, and tool permissions. [Install and try the skills](https://github.com/luka-duj/skills-for-better-work#install).

## How I work

Understand the work → identify credible value → build the smallest useful system → enable adoption → measure and improve.

My default is to simplify before automating, preserve evidence and uncertainty, and make ownership after launch visible early. Sometimes process and technology have to evolve together. That should be a bounded experiment, not an excuse to skip discovery.
