# Hi, I'm Luka Dujmovic.

**Enterprise Product Owner · AI-native Product Manager · Builder**

I lead internal product work where business priorities, how teams operate, and what technology can support all have to fit together. My strength is seeing how those pieces affect each other and turning that into choices about where to invest, what to simplify, and how to tell if the change worked.

I think the bigger opportunity with AI is to rethink how work gets done. I want to build systems that take some of the coordination and paperwork off people’s plates and give them more room to use their judgment. That means looking across teams at where decisions stall, who owns them, and what people need to trust a new way of working.

## What I build in my free time

In my free time, I build and share experiments around problems I’m curious about. I think about how teams decide what to build, how people get through municipal paperwork, and, every now and then, how to get a pixel-art shark to swim across my desktop.

- **[Skills for Better Work](https://github.com/luka-duj/skills-for-better-work)** — Six Agent Skills for product managers and workflow owners. The Better Work Loop helps teams decide whether to automate a workflow, scope the smallest useful test, and review the evidence before expanding it. Public alpha, built and tested in Codex. The skills can also be imported into Claude Code with small setup changes. [Install the skills](https://github.com/luka-duj/skills-for-better-work#install).
- **[Bro Shark for Codex](https://github.com/luka-duj/bro-shark)** — An animated pixel-art shark for Codex. The repository includes ready-to-import sprite sheets, animation previews, and a local installer. [See the install options](https://github.com/luka-duj/bro-shark#use-directly-in-codex).
- **[Falta un Papel](https://github.com/luka-duj/falta-un-papel)** — A Spanish-language plugin that finds official municipal forms and prepares a draft PDF or answers for the online form. You review and submit through the town hall’s official site. Alpha in development.

## How I got here

I started in **Customer Support**, where recurring requests taught me to spot workflow problems behind individual tickets. In **AI Solutions Operations**, I worked on internal AI and automation, built feedback and evaluation loops, and helped teams move beyond pilots.

I still look for the things I first saw in Support. When a process breaks, who has to pick up the slack? The answer often tells me where to start and who needs to be involved in changing it.

I started and lead **The AI Fellowship Madrid Chapter**, a local community for practical learning and responsible AI experimentation.
